useful video starts with a clear story.
Frame lab brings your recordings, notes, and
screenshots into one place. Start by organizing
the material you actually need, so every
scene has a purpose. Next, review the
cut, remove the mistakes and long pauses,
then check the words that remain, give
each visual one job, highlight a decision,
explain a change, or show the product
in action. Finally, deliver a video that
is easy to revise. Keep the narration
clear, the captions readable, and the graphics
consistent, when your brand changes, update the
design without rebuilding the story, that is
the workflow we are testing today.
